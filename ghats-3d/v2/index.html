How to use the Kashi journey

Boat journey: Select “Boat journey” to see a moving boat along an illustrative river-side curve between mapped landmarks. The boat pauses automatically at each location; select “Continue voyage” to proceed. Speed controls accelerate the sightseeing playback, not actual boating speed. Follow boat or choose wide camera. The photographic gallery can be opened at every stop, and the tour continues even if the map fails. The river path is scenic, NOT a surveyed channel, charter quote, distance measurement or safety guidance.

Select any major stop to fly to its mapped location. Use Guided journey for a user-controlled sequence between stops. Open each gallery and press Play if you want an automatic photo slideshow. The tour automatically pauses when you explore a gallery or manually choose a stop.

Map and Satellite show the real geographic context. 3D mapped buildings tilts the map and extrudes only footprints with mapped building-height data. This is not a surveyed architectural 3D scan. Dots mark only sourced positions. Straight connector lines and distance figures are lower-bound geographic references, NOT navigable river or walking routes. Unsurveyed traditional ghats remain searchable but are not given fabricated pins or distances.

For live boat/walking directions, seasonal accessibility or ritual access, consult local authorities and guides. Respect privacy and avoid photography of cremation ceremonies.
