How to use the Kashi journey

Select any major stop to fly to its mapped location. Use Guided journey for a user-controlled sequence between stops. Open each gallery and press Play if you want an automatic photo slideshow. The tour automatically pauses when you explore a gallery or manually choose a stop.

Map and Satellite show the real geographic context. 3D perspective tilts the map; it is not a surveyed architectural 3D scan. Dots mark only sourced positions. Straight connector lines and distance figures are lower-bound geographic references, NOT navigable river or walking routes. Unsurveyed traditional ghats remain searchable but are not given fabricated pins or distances.

For live boat/walking directions, seasonal accessibility or ritual access, consult local authorities and guides. Respect privacy and avoid photography of cremation ceremonies.
