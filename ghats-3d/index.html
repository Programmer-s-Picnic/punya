How to explore Kashi

Choose a ghat from the bottom ribbon or use any viewing mode.

  • Walk: W / A / S / D or arrow up/down + A/D to move; drag anywhere on the scene to turn the camera.
  • Mobile: Move the round joystick with your left thumb; drag on the right side of the screen to look around.
  • River cruise: Enjoy a moving boat-side view of the temples and steps.
  • Sunrise / evening: Change the atmosphere at the top right. Select a ghat to jump directly there.

Note: This journey spans Shooltankeshwar to the Ganga–Varuna Sangam through the 84 traditional riverfront ghats. It is a stylised representation, not a geospatial survey or navigation map. Sacred sites are presented respectfully.
